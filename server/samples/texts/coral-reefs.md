# Coral Reefs: Cities Under the Sea

## Coral Is Alive
Coral may look like rock, but it is made of tiny animals called polyps. Each polyp builds a hard skeleton of limestone around itself. Over many years, millions of skeletons pile up to make a reef.

## Partners in the Reef
Tiny algae live inside coral polyps. The algae use sunlight to make food and share it with the coral, and the coral gives the algae a safe home. The algae also give coral its bright colours.

## Why Reefs Need Care
Coral reefs are home to about one quarter of all sea creatures. When the water gets too warm, coral pushes its algae out and turns white. This is called coral bleaching. Keeping oceans clean and cool helps reefs stay healthy.
