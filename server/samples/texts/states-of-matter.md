# Solids, Liquids and Gases

## Three States of Matter
Everything around you is made of matter. Matter can be a solid, a liquid or a gas. A solid keeps its own shape. A liquid flows and takes the shape of its container. A gas spreads out to fill all the space it can.

## Heating and Cooling
Heating and cooling can change matter from one state to another. When ice is heated it melts into liquid water. When water is heated more it boils and turns into a gas called water vapour. When water vapour cools it condenses back into liquid drops.

## Tiny Particles
Matter is made of tiny particles. In a solid, the particles are packed tightly and only wiggle in place. In a liquid, they are close together but slide past each other. In a gas, the particles are far apart and zoom around quickly.
