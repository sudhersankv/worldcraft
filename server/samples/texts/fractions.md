# Fractions Are Fair Shares

## What Is a Fraction?
A fraction shows a part of a whole. When you cut a pizza into equal pieces, each piece is a fraction of the pizza. The pieces must be the same size for the fraction to be fair.

## Top and Bottom Numbers
A fraction has two numbers. The bottom number is the denominator. It tells how many equal parts the whole is split into. The top number is the numerator. It tells how many of those parts you have. In 3/4, you have three parts out of four.

## Comparing Fractions
When two fractions have the same denominator, the one with the bigger numerator is bigger. So 3/8 is more than 1/8. When the numerators are both 1, the fraction with the smaller denominator is bigger, because the whole is cut into fewer, larger pieces. So 1/2 is bigger than 1/4.
