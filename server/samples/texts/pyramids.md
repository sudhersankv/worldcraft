# The Pyramids of Ancient Egypt

## Tombs for Kings
The ancient Egyptians built pyramids as tombs for their kings, who were called pharaohs. They believed a pharaoh needed a safe resting place and treasures for the afterlife. The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for the pharaoh Khufu more than 4,500 years ago.

## Building with Stone
Workers cut huge blocks of limestone from quarries. They dragged the blocks on wooden sledges, and pouring water on the sand in front of the sledge made it easier to pull. They built long ramps of mud brick to raise the blocks higher and higher.

## Writing in Pictures
The Egyptians wrote with hieroglyphs, which are small pictures that stand for sounds and ideas. Scribes painted hieroglyphs on walls and wrote on paper made from the papyrus plant. The Rosetta Stone helped modern people learn to read hieroglyphs again.
